`bluffaktura` is Swedish for "fake invoice": a real scam letter styled as an
invoice, asking for SEK 2,870 to bankgiro 5014-4955. The handwritten
eviction notice is included as a hard case: browser OCR is not expected to
read cursive handwriting, and the app should say so instead of guessing.
