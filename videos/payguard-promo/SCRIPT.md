# SCRIPT — payguard-promo (under 3 minutes)

**Voice:** af_heart (Kokoro, local) · speed 0.95
**Voice direction:** Calm, warm, unhurried keynote delivery. Short sentences.

---

## Line 1 — Hook (Frame 1)

    This is a rent notice. This one is a scam. To an AI agent paying your bills, both are just text.

## Line 2 — The problem (Frame 2)

    One sentence, "pay instead to this account," can redirect a payment. And you can't build trust on a model never being fooled.

## Line 3 — Title (Frame 3)

    So we built PayGuard. The AI proposes. Only you approve.

## Line 4 — The breakthrough (Frame 4)

    We separate the part that thinks from the part that moves money. The agent can read and suggest, but it never holds a key.

## Line 5 — Signed mandate (Frame 5)

    You approve one exact payment. This amount, this account, once. PayGuard signs that snapshot with RSA P S S, over a SHA two fifty six digest. No document can change it.

## Line 6 — Scoped grant (Frame 6)

    The agent gets a grant for this one job. One execution. Gone in five minutes.

## Line 7 — Exact-match gateway (Frame 7)

    Its proposal goes to the gateway, the only code that moves money. Every field must match your signed approval. One difference, and it refuses.

## Line 8 — Exactly once (Frame 8)

    Twenty five identical requests, at the same moment. One transfer. Replay the job, and you get the same payment back.

## Line 9 — Signed receipts (Frame 9)

    Every decision is signed and hash chained. The export verifies with your own trusted keys.

## Line 10 — Photo notice (Frame 10)

    Snap a paper notice. It's read on your device. This one hides a redirect, and nothing moves.

## Line 11 — Laya pre-screen (Frame 11)

    Laya, a fast decision model, scores it first. Ninety four percent. It can send a payment to review, but never approve one.

## Line 12 — Attack Lab (Frame 12)

    In the Attack Lab, an agent that obeys every attack was fooled ten times out of ten.

## Line 13 — Defence in depth (Frame 13)

    The attacker got nothing. Laya caught nine. The review gate caught the tenth. And the gateway alone refused all ten.

## Line 14 — A real scam (Frame 14)

    Even a real scam letter, from the internet. Refused.

## Line 15 — Account change (Frame 15)

    A genuine change of bank? You confirm it, and the old approval dies.

## Line 16 — Engineering (Frame 16)

    Two hundred and sixteen tests. One container, ready for Cloud Run.

## Line 17 — Close (Frame 17)

    PayGuard. The AI proposes. The gateway decides.
